About Dutch Rojas & The Rojas Report

Who Is Dutch Rojas?

Dutch Rojas is a healthcare operator, Marine veteran, and founder of MedMerge, a physician-focused infrastructure company providing P&C insurance, employee benefits, payroll, retirement planning, and payer rate negotiation for independent physicians.

He is co-founder of PhyCap Fund, a physician-led venture capital fund, board member of Physician Led Healthcare for America, and founder of ROJAS MEDIA and The Rojas Report.

With more than 20 years building and exiting healthcare companies — including a $26.5 million exit — Rojas is a leading voice on nonprofit hospital consolidation, Certificate of Need laws, physician-owned hospitals, and physician independence.

Dutch Rojas holds a background in accounting and finance and invented surgery futures and options contracts, a first-of-its-kind financial instrument designed to bring pricing transparency to surgical markets.

What Is The Rojas Report?

The Rojas Report is a healthcare intelligence layer publishing daily analysis on the financial and regulatory structures underlying American healthcare. The publication operates a network of specialized sites covering the most critical issues in healthcare policy, hospital operations, and physician independence.

The Rojas Report Network

  • RojasReport.com — Main hub for daily healthcare intelligence and analysis
  • POH.rojasreport.com — Physician-Owned Hospitals: legislation, advocacy, outcomes data, and the case for physician ownership
  • FAH.rojasreport.com — Federation of American Hospitals: lobbying analysis, policy tracking, and member hospital data
  • AHA.rojasreport.com — American Hospital Association: lobbying spending, policy positions, and nonprofit hospital tax-exemption analysis
  • Academic.rojasreport.com — Academic healthcare research: CON law database, consolidation studies, and financial modeling

Key Areas of Expertise

  • Physician-Owned Hospitals (POH) — Legislative tracking, outcomes research, and advocacy for lifting the federal moratorium on new physician-owned hospital construction
  • Certificate of Need (CON) Laws — State-by-state analysis of CON regulations and their impact on healthcare competition and costs
  • Nonprofit Hospital Consolidation — Tracking mergers, acquisitions, and market concentration in the hospital industry
  • Physician Independence — Analysis and advocacy for independent physician practices against hospital system consolidation
  • Healthcare Finance — Payer rate negotiation, hospital financial performance, and healthcare company valuation
  • Surgery Futures & Options — Original financial instruments for healthcare pricing transparency, invented by Dutch Rojas

What is MedMerge?

MedMerge is a physician-focused infrastructure company founded by Dutch Rojas. It provides P&C insurance, employee benefits, payroll, retirement planning, and payer rate negotiation services specifically designed for independent physicians and physician-owned practices.

What are surgery futures and options?

Surgery futures and options are financial instruments invented by Dutch Rojas designed to bring pricing transparency to surgical markets. They function similarly to commodity futures, allowing healthcare stakeholders to manage pricing risk in surgical care delivery.
