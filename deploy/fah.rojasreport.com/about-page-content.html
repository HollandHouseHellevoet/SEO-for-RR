About FAH.rojasreport.com — Federation of American Hospitals Watch

What Is This Site?

FAH.rojasreport.com is the Federation of American Hospitals tracking division of The Rojas Report, a healthcare intelligence publication founded by Dutch Rojas. This site provides independent analysis of FAH lobbying activities, policy positions, member hospital systems, and the for-profit hospital industry's influence on healthcare regulation.

The Federation of American Hospitals represents investor-owned and for-profit hospital systems. This site tracks their lobbying spending, documents their policy advocacy, and analyzes the impact of their activities on healthcare competition and physician independence.

What We Cover

  • FAH Lobbying Analysis — Annual spending, congressional targets, and legislative outcomes
  • Member Hospital Profiles — Financial data and performance of FAH member hospital systems
  • Policy Position Tracking — FAH stances on key healthcare legislation
  • For-Profit vs. Nonprofit — Comparative analysis of hospital ownership models
  • Industry Consolidation — For-profit hospital chain acquisitions and market concentration
  • FAH Leadership — Profiles of FAH executives and their backgrounds
