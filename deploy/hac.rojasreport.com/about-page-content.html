About HAC Intelligence — Hospital Accountability & Consolidation

What Is This Site?

HAC.rojasreport.com is the hospital accountability and consolidation tracking division of The Rojas Report, a healthcare intelligence publication founded by Dutch Rojas. This site provides independent analysis of hospital mergers, market concentration, and the impact of consolidation on patients, physicians, and healthcare costs.

The American hospital industry has undergone decades of consolidation, concentrating market power among large health systems. HAC Intelligence documents these trends, analyzes their consequences, and holds hospital systems accountable for their financial practices and community obligations.

What We Cover

  • Hospital Mergers & Acquisitions — Tracking M&A activity across the hospital industry
  • Market Concentration — Regional and specialty-level analysis of hospital market power
  • Pricing Impact — How consolidation affects healthcare pricing for patients and employers
  • Community Obligations — Accountability for nonprofit hospital tax-exemption and community benefit
  • Physician Impact — How consolidation affects physician independence and practice economics
  • Antitrust Enforcement — FTC and DOJ activity in healthcare M&A
