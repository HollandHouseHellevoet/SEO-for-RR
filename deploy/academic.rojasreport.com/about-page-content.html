About Academic.rojasreport.com — Healthcare Research & Data

What Is This Site?

Academic.rojasreport.com is the research and academic analysis division of The Rojas Report, a healthcare intelligence publication founded by Dutch Rojas. This site publishes data-driven healthcare research with rigorous sourcing and methodology, serving as the evidence base for the broader Rojas Report network.

Content includes state-by-state Certificate of Need law databases, hospital consolidation impact studies, physician-owned hospital outcomes research, healthcare financial modeling, and original research on surgery futures and options contracts invented by Dutch Rojas.

What We Cover

  • Certificate of Need (CON) Law Database — Comprehensive state-by-state CON analysis and reform tracking
  • Hospital Consolidation Studies — Merger and acquisition data, market concentration analysis, and patient impact research
  • POH Outcomes Research — Comparative data on physician-owned vs. nonprofit hospital performance
  • Surgery Futures & Options — Original financial instruments for healthcare pricing transparency, invented by Dutch Rojas
  • Healthcare Financial Modeling — Hospital system valuations, payer rate analysis, and reimbursement data
  • Lobbying Impact Studies — Correlation analysis between healthcare lobbying spending and patient outcomes
  • Physician Workforce Economics — Data on physician independence, employment trends, and practice economics

About Dutch Rojas

Dutch Rojas is a healthcare operator, Marine veteran, and founder of MedMerge. He holds a background in accounting and finance and invented surgery futures and options contracts. With 20+ years building and exiting healthcare companies — including a $26.5 million exit — Rojas brings operational depth to academic-grade healthcare analysis.
