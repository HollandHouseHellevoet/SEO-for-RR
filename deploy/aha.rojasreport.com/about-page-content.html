About AHA.rojasreport.com — American Hospital Association Watch

What Is This Site?

AHA.rojasreport.com is the American Hospital Association tracking division of The Rojas Report, a healthcare intelligence publication founded by Dutch Rojas. This site provides independent analysis of AHA lobbying activities, policy positions, nonprofit hospital tax-exemption issues, and AHA's opposition to physician-owned hospitals and healthcare competition.

The American Hospital Association is the largest hospital trade association in the United States, representing nearly 5,000 hospitals and health systems. This site examines how AHA shapes healthcare regulation, often in ways that limit competition, restrict physician-owned hospitals, and protect incumbent hospital systems.

What We Cover

  • AHA Lobbying Analysis — Annual spending, congressional targets, and legislative outcomes
  • Nonprofit Hospital Tax-Exemption — Community benefit analysis vs. tax revenue forgone
  • AHA vs. Physician-Owned Hospitals — AHA's campaign against POH expansion
  • Policy Position Tracking — AHA stances on Certificate of Need, competition, and consolidation
  • Member Hospital Network — Geographic and financial data on AHA members
  • AHA Leadership — Profiles of AHA executives and board members
  • Hospital Consolidation — AHA's role in enabling or defending consolidation
