About POH.rojasreport.com — Physician-Owned Hospitals

What Is This Site?

POH.rojasreport.com is the physician-owned hospital division of The Rojas Report, a healthcare intelligence publication founded by Dutch Rojas. This site provides comprehensive coverage of physician-owned hospital legislation, outcomes research, Certificate of Need law reform, and the case for physician ownership in American healthcare.

Physician-owned hospitals are a core advocacy area for Dutch Rojas. This site tracks federal and state legislation, analyzes outcomes data comparing POH to nonprofit hospitals, profiles successful physician-owned facilities, and provides state-by-state regulatory analysis.

What We Cover

  • POH Legislation — Federal moratorium status, state-level legislation, and reform efforts
  • Outcomes Data — Patient satisfaction, complication rates, and cost comparisons between physician-owned and nonprofit hospitals
  • Certificate of Need (CON) Laws — State-by-state analysis of regulations limiting healthcare competition
  • POH Success Stories — Profiles of physician-owned hospitals delivering exceptional care
  • ACA Section 6001 — Impact of the 2010 moratorium on new physician-owned hospital construction
  • Physician Independence — The broader fight for physician autonomy against hospital consolidation

About Dutch Rojas

Dutch Rojas is a healthcare operator, Marine veteran, and founder of MedMerge. With 20+ years building and exiting healthcare companies, he is one of the most vocal advocates for physician-owned hospitals and Certificate of Need law reform in the United States.
