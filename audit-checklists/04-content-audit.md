# Content Audit Checklist

Evaluate the quality, authority, and effectiveness of content across all domains. Content is the foundation of both traditional SEO and AI/LLM citation.

**Apply to each domain:** RojasReport.com | POH | FAH | AHA | Academic

---

## E-E-A-T Assessment (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust)

### Experience
- [ ] Content demonstrates first-hand experience in healthcare operations
- [ ] Real-world examples from Dutch Rojas's 20+ years in healthcare
- [ ] Case studies from MedMerge, PhyCap Fund, or other ventures
- [ ] Personal insights from building and exiting healthcare companies

### Expertise
- [ ] Author credentials clearly displayed (healthcare operator, Marine veteran, accounting/finance background)
- [ ] Technical depth appropriate for each subdomain:
  - POH: deep regulatory and legislative knowledge
  - FAH: financial analysis of hospital federation activities
  - AHA: policy analysis with data backing
  - Academic: research-grade analysis with citations
- [ ] Content demonstrates subject matter mastery, not surface-level summaries

### Authority
- [ ] Author bio on every article with credentials and photo
- [ ] About page on each domain with comprehensive background
- [ ] External citations from other healthcare media (quotes, references)
- [ ] Speaking engagements, media appearances documented
- [ ] Published in recognized healthcare outlets

### Trust
- [ ] Contact information easily accessible
- [ ] Privacy policy present
- [ ] Terms of service present
- [ ] Sources cited for all factual claims
- [ ] Corrections policy (if any previous errors have been corrected)
- [ ] HTTPS on all pages
- [ ] No misleading headlines or clickbait

## Content Quality

- [ ] **No thin content** — every page has substantive, valuable content (300+ words minimum for articles)
- [ ] **No duplicate content:**
  - Same article not published on multiple subdomains
  - Content not copied from other sources without attribution
  - No auto-generated or spun content
- [ ] **Content freshness:**
  - Published dates visible on all articles
  - Last-updated dates on evergreen content
  - Outdated content either updated or clearly dated
  - Regular publishing cadence maintained
- [ ] **Comprehensive coverage:**
  - Topics covered in depth, not just surface-level
  - Related subtopics addressed
  - Common follow-up questions answered within the same piece
- [ ] **Readability:**
  - Writing level appropriate for audience (healthcare professionals + informed public)
  - Short paragraphs and sentences
  - Technical terms explained when first used
  - Scannable structure (headings, bullets, bold key points)

## Content-Search Intent Alignment

- [ ] **Informational content** answers "what/why/how" queries directly
  - Example: "What is a Certificate of Need law?" is answered clearly
- [ ] **Analytical content** provides unique perspective and data
  - Example: "How AHA lobbying spending has changed over 10 years"
- [ ] **News/update content** is timely and factual
  - Example: "New physician-owned hospital legislation introduced in Senate"
- [ ] **Advocacy content** clearly labeled as opinion/editorial
- [ ] **Target keywords identified** for each piece of content
- [ ] **Search intent matched** — informational queries get information, not sales pitches

## Content Structure for AI Citation

- [ ] **Direct answers** in the first paragraph (AI systems extract these)
- [ ] **Bullet points and numbered lists** used for key facts (30%+ more likely to be cited by LLMs)
- [ ] **FAQ format** where appropriate (questions AI users actually ask)
- [ ] **Summary paragraphs** at end of long articles ("In summary..." or "Key takeaways:")
- [ ] **Data and statistics** included with sources
- [ ] **Definitions** provided for key terms (gets cited as reference)
- [ ] **Comparative analysis** (e.g., "physician-owned vs. nonprofit hospitals") — AI loves structured comparisons

## Content Inventory

Create a spreadsheet or document listing for each domain:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| URL | Full page URL |
| Title | Page title |
| Word Count | Length of content |
| Published Date | When first published |
| Last Updated | Most recent update |
| Target Keyword | Primary keyword targeted |
| Search Intent | Informational / Transactional / Navigational |
| Traffic | Monthly organic traffic (from GSC) |
| Ranking | Current position for target keyword |
| Quality Score | 1-5 subjective quality rating |
| Action Needed | Keep / Update / Consolidate / Remove |

- [ ] **Keyword gap analysis** — target keywords you rank for vs. competitors
- [ ] **Topic gaps** — important healthcare topics with no coverage:

  - [ ] Hospital consolidation impact data
  - [ ] Payer rate negotiation guides
  - [ ] Healthcare venture capital landscape
